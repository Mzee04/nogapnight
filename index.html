    About

    Mosquito nets only protect when they fully cover the sleeping place. Gaps and a poor fit let mosquitoes bite. The World Health Organization recommends using a mosquito net when sleeping where malaria is present, and lists insecticide-treated nets as a core malaria intervention.

    How it was built: phone photo → Aholo Lux3D → GLB → this app.

    GitHub repo · Report PDF · [video coming]

    Honest note: a first estimate and a practical aid, not a certified health device. Net sizes are standard LLIN presets (check your net's label), and the drape is a geometric estimate, not cloth physics. Sleep under a treated net every night and fix holes.
